What the
app does.

Four tabs. One AI. Everything you need in your pocket.

01

Talks to you naturally

Hold to speak. It responds in real-time with a voice you pick. Not a text-to-speech robot — a voice that fits the personality you chose.

02

Remembers everything

Past conversations, your preferences, your schedule, your goals, that restaurant you mentioned three months ago. It never forgets.

03

Works while you don't

Say "research this" and walk away. It spawns background agents that run for minutes or hours, then ping you when done. You never wait.

04

Sees what you send

Drop a photo — it identifies it. Upload a PDF — it extracts the data. Share a screenshot — it reads it. Multi-modal from day one.

05

Fixes itself

Every 10 minutes it scans for problems. Every 12 hours a senior auditor reviews. It gets measurably smarter every week with zero intervention.

06

Becomes yours

Choose its name. Choose its personality. Import your data from ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. It starts knowing you — then never stops learning.
